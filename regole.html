REGULATION COCO MC

Screen Sharing Policy and Information
1 If you log out while in the checking phase, it will attempt to delete files, empty the recycle bin, delete browser history, end some Windows processes or modify some game specific logs before sharing, this will result in TempBan from Coco Mc for 15 days.
2 Coco Mc staff reserves the right to initiate screen sharing at any time. moment, in order to supervise compliance with the regulation. The refusal of this practice will result in TempBan from Coco Mc for 15 days.
3 All screen sharing is recorded by the staff conducting the review. Players can request an administrator to review them if they wish.
4 Staff members will not access any private information. If you wish report a staff member because you believe they have reviewed your private information, contact an administrator.
5 Staff members will be able to use additional programs to be able to identify any type of unauthorized modification, process or program. If you have any concerns about the program installed, feel free to ask the staffer conducting the check for information.
1. General Regulations
1 You cannot use any kind of Hacked Client, Ghost Client, Injection Client, External Client and Illegal Mods, etc. Modifications to any client or mod that create an advantage over other players are not allowed. Use of such subterfuge will result in a TempBan from Coco Mc for 20 days.
2 You may not use any kind of Autoclicker, Macro, etc. Anything that is not considered a physical click will be considered an Autoclicker and will result in TempBan from Coco Mc for 20 days, Jitter click and drag click are not allowed.
3 Using a Debounce Time lower than 10ms is absolutely forbidden and will result in the TempBan from Coco Mc for 20 days.
4 It is absolutely forbidden to exploit any bug and it is mandatory to report it to the Coco Mc staff: This action will result in TempBan from Coco Mc for 3 days.
5 The /report command should only be used to report a player, for other issues you feel feel free to open a ticket on our Discord server: any improper use will be punished by the staff.
6 It is absolutely forbidden to build any kind of Lag Machine: violation of this rule will be punishable based on the severity of the situation and at the discretion of the staff.
7 It is forbidden to have more than one account connected to the network except for members of the same household. family members such as brothers, sisters or parents, in the event of transgression the penalty is a Permanent Ban.
8 Environmental griefing is prohibited, this includes wanton environmental destruction, figure building obscene with blocks: if you practice it you will receive a punishment from the staff based on the severity of the situation
2. General Chat Regulations
1 Bigotry, toxicity and hate speech, includes the use of any racial or homophobic term in chat and are not tolerated in any way, any attempt to circumvent this rule will result in a TempMute on Coco Mc for 24 hours.
2 Insulting or threatening another player or staff member is not tolerated in any way, any attempt bypassing this rule will result in a TempMute on Coco Mc for 4 hours.
3 Advertising in chat is not tolerated. Advertising is the act of posting IPs, other server names and link in public chat in the hope that players will join, such action will result in a temporary ban from Coco Mc of 1 day.
4 Doxing, ddosing or swatting is not tolerated on Coco Mc. If someone threatens you in private messages, contact a staff member to verify. Also any kind of malicious joke on this topic is punishable by a Permanent Ban from Coco Mc
5 Telling someone to kill themselves is strictly forbidden. If a player sends you a private message with someone such as contact a staff member. This action will result in Coco Mc being TempMuted for 48 hours.
6 Using public chat to impersonate staff or famous people when you are not is not allowed, such action will result in a TempMute on Coco Mc for 1 day.
7 Using an inappropriate nickname or skin is punishable by a Temporary Ban from ServerName for a maximum of 24 hours
8 8. Users are advised to write in the official language of the country where the server is located, i.e. Indian except for English which is considered a secondary language, in case of transgression the staff is authorized to change for a period of time between 3 and 12 hours
