Unique Modes

You will be able to find special modes that cannot be found in other minecraft servers !

Survival

Are you bored of playing singleplayer? Enter our vanilla to not miss anything, spectacular events, fantastic feature you can't even imagine! To play this mode at its best, invite as many friends as possibl e and have fun all together! Have fun!

Prison

a solo mode, objective, reach the highest prestige, climb the mine by selling more and more, improve your pickaxe to satellite levels, arrive first in the top bal and top token and brag to your friends! Participate in the fantastic daily events!

Fazioni

A team mode, the goal is to get first in F-Top, to win the 1st place prize together with your faction you will have to attack the enemy factions and steal their spawners, then place them in your territories and make your faction rise! Good luck!

BedWars

If you like PvP, you will love this awesome mode! The goal is to survive, but you can also play in teams. To survive you will have to kill everyone your opponents permanently, to do this you will have to destroy the bed on their island. Once the bed is destroyed the player becomes mortal and you will have no choice but to do clean sweep.
